# After the party - what we learned

From `result.json` (written Sunday morning) and `roomcalc.py tally` (`tally.output.json`).

- 21 people came against 20.8 expected: turnout 1.01 of prediction. The 0.92 default held.
- **Ran out:** chicken skewers at 16:20, samosas at 16:45. True demand was higher than
  planned; the kids went for both. Next time +1 pack of skewers and ask grandma for 30.
- **About right:** sausage rolls, rice-paper rolls, corn cups. Their leftovers were no more
  than pack rounding.
- **Too much:** wraps (35% left), fruit (30% left), cake (6 slices left). Next time one pack
  of wraps fewer, 3 kg of fruit, a 20-slice cake.
- Crowd appetite on the uncensored dishes: 1.13x the defaults - this crowd eats a bit more
  than the table. Use 1.1 on mains next year.
- Nobody needed the reserve crisps; they keep.
