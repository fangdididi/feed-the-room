# Portions, yields and supplies

Starting points for one adult. Children under 12 count as half, teens as 1.2, toddlers as 0
(they usually share or bring their own). Adjust with your own tally numbers.

`per_person` in the plan file means: what one adult takes **if this were the only dish in its
course**. The helper scales it when guests have several choices:

| Safe dishes in the course | Each is taken at |
|---|---|
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 60% |
| 3 | 45% |
| 4 or more | 35% |

Dishes marked `"each": true` (cake slice, boxed lunch, favour bag) skip this: one per person.

## Meals (single-dish amounts, cooked, on the plate)

| Food | Per adult |
|---|---|
| Meat or fish, main course | 170-220 g (6-8 oz); buffets and barbecues the high end |
| Meat, bought raw, boneless | about 1.3-1.4x the cooked amount (yield 0.7-0.75) |
| Bone-in chicken, ribs | yield 0.45-0.55 |
| Rice, pasta, potatoes (cooked) | 150-200 g |
| Vegetables | 120-170 g |
| Green salad | 1 cup as a side, 2-3 cups as the main |
| Soup | 250-300 ml |
| Sandwiches / wraps | 1 whole sandwich or 3 small pieces |
| Pizza | 3 slices (12-inch / 30 cm, 8 slices), teens 4 |
| Sushi | 8-10 pieces as a main |
| Bread | 1-2 rolls |
| Dumplings | 12-15 as a main |

## Snacks and receptions

| Situation | Pieces per adult |
|---|---|
| Finger food before a full meal, about 1 hour | 4-6 |
| Reception of up to 2 hours, no meal after | 8-12 |
| Finger food is the meal, 2-3 hours | 12-15, across at least 6 kinds |
| Each extra hour | about half the first hour |

People eat most in the first hour. Passed food is eaten less than a self-serve table; an open
bar keeps people longer and eating more.

## Desserts and fruit

| Food | Per adult |
|---|---|
| Cake | 1 slice (`each`) |
| Cupcakes, cookies | 1-2 |
| Fruit platter | 120-150 g usable; watermelon yield 0.5-0.6, grapes 0.95 |
| Ice cream | 120 ml |

## Service buffer (added on top)

| Style | Buffer |
|---|---|
| Plated, boxed | 5% |
| Family-style | 10% |
| Buffet | 15% |
| Grazing table / stations | 18% |

## Drinks and supplies (per expected person)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Drinks | 2 servings in the first hour, 1 per hour after; x1.25 in heat |
| Water | always available, on top of the above |
| Ice | 0.5 kg (x1.5 in heat) for drinks; extra for coolers |
| Plates | 1.5 |
| Cups | 2 |
| Napkins | 3 |
| Cutlery | 1.5 sets if needed |

## Pack rounding

Always round up to whole packs, then look at the leftover the rounding creates. If one dish
rounds up by more than half a pack, try a smaller pack or move part of its share to another dish.
